# Part Three. The Commandments

Notes keyed to the printed pages of *Creator-Aware AI*. Each entry gives the page, the words in the text, and the source.

**p. 137**, "process, never by the systems they govern"  
The Ten Commandments of Creator-Aware AI (unpublished, late 2025; first published in this book): the opening statement, the ten commandments with their explanations, prompt lines and review checks, and the precedence rule from "Where the commandments pull against each other", quoted as written. The explanations follow the source with one change: the seventh's "The rules are revisited" is given as "They are revisited", so that the set is called commandments throughout.

**p. 137**, "This is the original set, version 1"  
Anthony Doyle, "When the Creation Knows the Creator: Building AI that Respects Humanity", 22 October 2025, which set out the charter the commandments are distilled from. The commandments were written shortly afterwards and are first published in this book.

**p. 141**, "in control while the conflict is resolved"  
See the note for page 137.
