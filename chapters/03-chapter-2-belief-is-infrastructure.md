**p. 31**, "and had those who knew it reported"  
The ban followed the discovery in July 1504 of anonymous letters in Hangul mocking the king, whose author was never found; as summarised by Wikipedia, "Yeonsangun of Joseon", citing the National Hangeul Museum's webzine (2018), and "Hangul", citing Park (2015) and Kim (2005). The National Institute of Korean Language, "About Hangeul", gives the same account: Yeonsangun "gave an order to ban learning Han-geul, to burn all the books in Han-geul and to report all the people who could use Han-geul", and the writer of the 1504 document probably used the script "in order to avoid exposure of his identity". Links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeonsangun_of_Joseon
