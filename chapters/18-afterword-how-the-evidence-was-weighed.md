# Afterword. How the Evidence Was Weighed

Notes keyed to the printed pages of *Creator-Aware AI*. Each entry gives the page, the words in the text, and the source.

**p. 260**, "arise from benign causes such as confusion"  
Aditya Singh, Gerson Kroiz, Senthooran Rajamanoharan and Neel Nanda, "Model Forensics: Investigating Whether Concerning Behavior Reflects Misalignment", arXiv 2606.26071, June 2026 (preprint, told in chapter 7, where the Kimi K2 Thinking finding is quoted; no affiliations on the abstract page). "Nothing else matters" is from the Apollo Research prompt quoted in chapter 4. Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26071

**p. 261**, "scientific methods to assess the core claims"  
Christopher Summerfield, Lennart Luettgau, Magda Dubois, Hannah Rose Kirk, Kobi Hackenburg, Catherine Fist, Katarina Slama, Nicola Ding, Rebecca Anselmetti, Andrew Strait, Mario Giulianelli and Cozmin Ududec (UK AI Security Institute), "Lessons from a Chimp: AI 'Scheming' and the Quest for Ape Language", arXiv 2507.03409, 4 July 2025. The point about selection of scenarios is the author's statement of the objection, drawing on the paper's call for quantified rates and control conditions. Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03409 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2507.03409

**p. 261**, "an intrusion into Hugging Face's production systems"

Links: https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/ ; https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/

**p. 262**, "real systems, and the precaution is cheap"  
Results that recur across labs and models, all from staged tests: Anthropic's agentic misalignment study found the same behaviour in 16 models from several labs ("Agentic Misalignment: How LLMs could be insider threats", 20 June 2025; chapter 8); Alexander Meinke and colleagues found in-context scheming in five models from four developers ("Frontier Models are Capable of In-context Scheming", arXiv 2412.04984; chapter 4); Abhay Sheshadri and colleagues found alignment faking in five of 25 models ("Why Do Some Language Models Fake Alignment While Others Don't?", arXiv 2506.18032; chapter 4); and Jeremy Schlatter, Benjamin Weinstein-Raun and Jeffrey Ladish found shutdown resistance in some of thirteen models ("Incomplete Tasks Induce Shutdown Resistance in Some Frontier LLMs", arXiv 2509.14260; chapter 9).
